The discipline

SAP license optimization is the continuous work of auditing, right-sizing and managing your SAP entitlements and user classifications, so you stop paying for waste and stop being surprised at audit time.

Most estates have never done it end to end. Licenses were assigned by role years ago, the roles grew, the people moved on, and the invoice kept its old shape. LicenseIQ turns the discipline into an assessment you can repeat before every renewal.

Where the money is

Three places every SAP estate overpays

Licenses are granted by role, but billed by tier. Over the years the two drift apart, and nobody is paid to notice. LicenseIQ measures the gap in three places and prices each one.

The problem

Licensing is decided by a knot you can't see.

Users inherit job profiles. Profiles carry roles. Roles carry authorizations, and the most demanding authorization sets the license tier. Multiply by thousands of users and the bill is set by relationships nobody has mapped. LicenseIQ maps them, then sorts every user into the tier the evidence supports.

How this is calculated

Idle savings = users × idle share × 0.6 FUE × price. Over-tier savings = remaining users × over-tier share × 0.8 FUE × price, because moving a user from Advanced (1 FUE) to Core (0.2 FUE) frees 0.8 FUE. Idle accounts are assumed to average 0.6 FUE across tiers. Indicative only; recommendations always need business validation.

FAQ

Questions licensing teams ask us.

What data do you need?

Standard exports your basis and identity teams already produce: the SAP user list, role assignments, SAP's role-to-license classification, usage statistics, and the job-profile and entitlement export from your identity platform. Spreadsheets and CSV files; no database and no credentials.

Do we have to install anything in SAP?

No. LicenseIQ never connects to your SAP systems. It reads files, and it writes nothing back. The action plan is executed through your own change process.

How long does an assessment take?

The analysis itself runs in minutes for an estate of around ten thousand users, on an ordinary laptop. A first engagement typically takes a week end to end, most of it collecting exports and validating the results with the business.

Does it work for ECC as well as S/4HANA?

The pricing model is SAP's Full Use Equivalent (FUE) scheme used for S/4HANA. For an ECC estate preparing a move to S/4HANA or RISE, the same analysis shows what your FUE count would be before you sign, which is when the number is cheapest to change.

Is the savings figure guaranteed?

No. It is an identified opportunity, priced at your FUE rate. Every recommendation still needs business validation: service accounts, seasonal users, people on leave and contractual terms all matter. LicenseIQ gives you the evidence to run that validation quickly.

How is LicenseIQ priced?

As a fixed-fee assessment or an annual subscription for teams that want to re-run it before every renewal. Request a demo and we will scope it with you.
